Shotsmith Privacy Policy

Shotsmith: Screenshot Editor, for iOS and Android. Effective date: 24 November 2026.

EnglishTürkçe

In short: Shotsmith edits your screenshots on your device. We have no accounts, no servers of our own and no analytics. Your images and any text recognized in them never leave your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses RevenueCat to check the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Those two services receive limited device and usage data, described below.

1. Who we are

Shotsmith ("the app") is published by the Shotsmith developer ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the limited processing described here. Contact: magodeouz@gmail.com.

2. Data processed only on your device

The following data is processed only on your phone. We never receive it, and it is not sent to any third party:

  • Screenshots and images you capture, import from your photo library or open through the "Open in Shotsmith" Shortcuts action (iOS), plus your edits, annotations, backgrounds and stitched long screenshots.
  • Text recognition (OCR) and QR/barcode reading. This runs entirely on-device, using Apple Vision on iOS and Google ML Kit's on-device text and barcode recognition on Android. Recognized text stays on your device.
  • Screen recordings and, if you turn it on, microphone audio recorded with them.
  • Capture history and settings, such as pins, filters and auto-cleanup preferences, stored in the app's private storage.

Images leave the app only when you choose to save them to your photo library, copy them to the clipboard or share them through the system share sheet. In that case, they go to the destination you pick.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version of the app shows a short rewarded video ad before each save or export. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers: on iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, otherwise app- or vendor-scoped identifiers; on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (coarse) location.
  • Device information, such as model, operating system, language and network type.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed, taps and video views, and diagnostic and performance information.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, limit how often an ad is shown, and prevent fraud. If you consent, it also uses the data to personalize ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any personalized ads are shown. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you buy "Remove Ads", no further ads are requested or shown.

RevenueCat (in-app purchase)

The optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. To validate the purchase and restore it on your other devices, we use RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, your purchase history (product, date, price, store transaction/receipt data), and basic device and app information such as platform, OS version, app version and country/store. This ID is not linked to your name or email. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we do not use

We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or other tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal data. Apart from the ad and purchase processing described above, we do not share data with anyone.

5. Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): you can allow or deny tracking when the app asks, and change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent form (EEA/UK/Switzerland): you can review or change your ad consent at any time from the privacy options entry in the app's Settings screen.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Remove ads: the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase stops all ad requests.
  • Permissions: you can revoke any permission in your device settings. Related features will stop working, and the rest of the app keeps working.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Images, recordings, history and settings stay on your device until you delete them, until auto-cleanup removes them (if enabled), or until you uninstall the app. Files you saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them.
  • Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies. See the Google links above.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to validate and restore your purchase, and to meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete this data by emailing magodeouz@gmail.com. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for your store order ID so we can find the record. Deleting it means the purchase may need to be restored again from the store.

7. Children

Shotsmith is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, contact us and we will help delete it.

8. Security and international transfers

Data sent to Google and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). These providers may process data in the United States and other countries, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email magodeouz@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We process ad data on the basis of your consent where the law requires it, and otherwise on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding the free app. We process purchase data to provide the product you bought.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
